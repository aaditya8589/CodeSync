title: Add Two Numbers
difficulty: Easy
position: 1
=== statement
Read two integers and print their sum.

A warm-up to check how input and output work here: read from standard input, print to standard output, nothing else.
=== input
One line with two integers `a` and `b`.
=== output
Print `a + b`.
=== constraints
-10^18 <= a, b <= 10^18
=== sample
3 4
--- expected
7
=== sample
-10 25
--- expected
15
